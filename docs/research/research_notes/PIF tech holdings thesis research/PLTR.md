## Q1. How does the company make money?

### Takeaway
Palantir is now about half government and half commercial, and 84% US. Q3 2026 revenue was $2.313B (+96% YoY), led by US commercial at $984M (+148%), which passed US government ($953M, +96%) for the first time. International is the weak spot: international commercial grew 21% YoY and only 1% QoQ.

### Cited Findings
**Revenue mix, Q3 2026 vs Q3 2025**
- Total revenue $2,313.4M vs $1,181.1M (+96% YoY, +20% QoQ). [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000048/pltr-20260930.htm); [Q3 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000045/a2026q3ex991pressrelease.htm)
- Government $1,145.5M (Q3 2025: $632.7M); Commercial $1,167.9M (Q3 2025: $548.4M). That is about 49.5% government / 50.5% commercial, with government +81% and commercial +113% YoY (my calc). [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000048/pltr-20260930.htm)
- United States $1,937.2M (84% of revenue, up from 75%); rest of world $376.2M (16%, down from 25%). Rest of world grew about 26% YoY (my calc). [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000048/pltr-20260930.htm)

[10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000048/pltr-20260930.htm); [Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/palantir-q3-fy-2026-earnings-us-commercial-revenue-overtakes-us-government/)

**Guidance (2026-11-02)**
- Q4 2026 revenue $2.649B to $2.653B (about +15% QoQ); FY2026 revenue $8.530B to $8.534B (+91%), up from $8.150B to $8.158B; FY2026 US commercial revenue above $3.559B (at least +143%); FY2026 adjusted operating income $5.245B to $5.249B; FY2026 adjusted FCF $4.8B to $5.0B. [Q3 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000045/a2026q3ex991pressrelease.htm); [Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/palantir-q3-fy-2026-earnings-us-commercial-revenue-overtakes-us-government/)
- FY2026 revenue growth guidance has gone from 61% (February) to 91% (November). [TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/palantir-is-down-15-in-a-year-anything-short-of-a-blowout-is-a-sell)

### Inferences
- Revenue (+96%) is growing far faster than bookings (+6% TCV) and forward backlog (RDV +4% QoQ, RPO flat QoQ). Either Palantir is converting short contracts to revenue quickly (management's explanation: shorter-duration Sovereign AI deals) or forward growth will slow. This is the single most important metric gap to watch.
- Concentration is moving up, not down: US share went from 75% to 84%, and top 20 customers are roughly 40% of revenue.
- The business is a US story. International commercial at +1% QoQ is close to flat.

### Gaps
- Total RDV for the company comes only from the call summary, not the filed release.
- Exact NDR definition (company-wide vs. US commercial) not checked in the investor presentation.

## Q2. What do they sell, and what is the current sentiment?

### Takeaway
Four platforms: Gotham (government/defense), Foundry (commercial data operations), AIP (AI layer on Foundry/Gotham that runs LLMs and agents on a customer's "ontology"), and Apollo (deployment). Defense sentiment is strong (Army $10B enterprise agreement, Maven as a program of record), commercial sentiment is driven by AIP bootcamps and a newer "Sovereign AI" pitch. Customer reviews are positive on capability, negative on cost, opacity and lock-in.

### Cited Findings
**Commercial (AIP, bootcamps, Sovereign AI)**
- Management cited pilot-to-contract conversions in Q3: industrial materials ~$3M pilot to $29M ACV; athletic company $1.6M pilot to ~$21M ACV; home appliance $3M to $15M ACV; apparel $2.5M pilot to $11.5M ACV. Karp said deals now start around $25M rather than $250K. [Yahoo call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/palantir-technologies-inc-pltr-q3-050513873.html)
- "Sovereign AI" bootcamps: Karp said customers arrive convinced their IP is at risk with current setups; CFO Glazer said Sovereign AI is "only one quarter old" and several eight-figure Q3 deals had shorter durations. [Yahoo call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/palantir-technologies-inc-pltr-q3-050513873.html)
- Karp argued AI labs push businesses to give up data sovereignty and backed open-weight models; he again called for nationalizing AI labs. [CNBC, 2026-11-02](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/palantir-pltr-earnings-q3-2026.html)

**Government and defense**

## Q3. Who are the competitors, and what is the sentiment around them?

### Takeaway
Most comparisons place Databricks as the main data/AI rival, Microsoft Fabric as the most dangerous on price because of bundling, and frontier AI labs (Anthropic, OpenAI) as a new threat raised by both analysts and Michael Burry. Peer quality on this question is weak (mostly blogs); treat as qualitative.

### Cited Findings
- Databricks is stronger as an AI engineering foundation; Palantir is stronger at putting agents into operational workflows. Databricks agent products: Agent Bricks, Genie. [B-EYE buyer's guide](https://b-eye.com/blog/databricks-vs-palantir/)

- C3.ai is shrinking: Q1 revenue fell 19% YoY to $70.3M (period per Nasdaq article). [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/c3ai-faces-hard-competition-enterprise-ai-can-it-defend-turf)
- Snowflake Cortex, Salesforce Agentforce and ServiceNow agents are cited as additional competition; many enterprises are expected to run several vendors. [IBTimes AU](https://ibtimes.com.au/top-5-best-palantir-competitors-2026-led-databricks-snowflake-microsoft-fabric-data-ai-platforms-1865435)
- AI labs: on the Q3 call, D.A. Davidson's Gil Luria asked how Palantir persuades CEOs given that about half of Anthropic's customers work directly with the lab; the call summary lists labs as a competitive risk. [Yahoo call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/palantir-technologies-inc-pltr-q3-050513873.html)
- Burry argued (in a since-deleted post) that Anthropic is capturing enterprise AI spend, comparing Anthropic ARR growth from $9B to $30B in months with Palantir's ~two decades to reach $5B revenue; PLTR fell about 8% after that post **[OLDER, spring 2026]**. [Yahoo, 2026-04-10](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/michael-burry-holds-palantir-short-183800508.html)
- Wedbush's Dan Ives called the rivals-displacing-Palantir narrative "fictional." [Blockport](https://blockport.io/latest-news/burry-keeps-long-palantir-puts-despite-trump-praise-wedbush/)
- Jefferies (after Q3, per Stocktwits headline) sees better AI bets in MSFT, ORCL and SNOW. [Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/pltr-stock-gets-price-target-hikes-after-another-top-line-blowout-but-jefferies-sees-better-ai-bets-in-msft-orcl-snow-shares/cNGX4OMR95v) (headline only, not read in full)

- Palantir's numbers (US commercial +148%) do not yet show share loss. The competitive risk is mostly about the future: whether labs and hyperscalers absorb the "orchestration layer" Palantir sells.

## Q5. Cash vs. debt

### Takeaway
Net cash of about $10.3B to $10.6B, no borrowings, cash up $3.4B since December 2025, FY2026 adjusted FCF guided at $4.8B to $5.0B. Buybacks are negligible.

### Cited Findings
- Cash and equivalents $3,619.4M + marketable securities $6,942.1M = ~$10.56B at 2026-09-30, up from ~$7.18B at 2025-12-31. [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000048/pltr-20260930.htm)
- Press release states $10.4B of cash, cash equivalents and US Treasury securities (not reconciled to the balance sheet). [Q3 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000045/a2026q3ex991pressrelease.htm)
- Debt: $500M revolving credit facility maturing 2027-03-31, no borrowings outstanding at 2026-09-30. [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000048/pltr-20260930.htm)
- Q3 cash from operations $1.205B (52% margin); adjusted FCF $1.216B (53% margin). FY2026 adjusted FCF guide raised to $4.8B to $5.0B from $4.5B to $4.7B. [Q3 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001321655/000132165526000045/a2026q3ex991pressrelease.htm)
