Most recent reported quarter: Q3 2026 (quarter ended 2026-09-30, reported 2026-11-03). The Q3 2026 10-Q and shareholder letter are the primary sources. Figures dated before 2026-05-12 are flagged "(older than 6 months)".

Check on the fund app's Yahoo baseline (pulled 2026-11-12):
- Trailing revenue $1.18B: confirmed. Q4'25 $282.9M + Q1'26 $292.0M + Q2'26 $298.5M + Q3'26 $309.0M = $1,182.4M (company letters, cited below).
- Revenue growth 13.7%: confirmed. Q3 2026 revenue $309.0M vs $271.7M = +13.7%.
- Total cash $1.37B: matches cash and equivalents ($1,239.6M) plus short-term investments ($134.3M) = $1,373.9M. It leaves out $110.1M of long-term investments.

## 1. How does Duolingo make money? (Q3 2026 numbers)

### Takeaway
Subscriptions are 87% of revenue ($270.0M of $309.0M in Q3 2026) and the only line growing fast (+18%). Advertising, the Duolingo English Test and in-app purchases are flat or shrinking. Users are growing faster than money: DAUs rose 29% while bookings rose 12%.

Total $309.0M (+14%) ([Q3 2026 shareholder letter, SEC 8-K, 2026-11-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm))

### Inferences
- Paid penetration is flat. Paid subscribers were 8.6% of MAUs in Q3 2026 (13.1/153.1) vs 8.5% a year earlier (11.5/135.3), computed from the letter. Subscriber growth (+14%) is about half of DAU growth (+29%). So the new DAUs are mostly free users so far.
- Engagement is rising: DAU/MAU went from 37.3% to 42.4% year over year (computed). This supports the "retention" explanation.
- Subscription bookings (+16%) are growing faster than total bookings (+12%) because in-app purchases (-38%) and ads (-1%) shrank. The in-app purchase decline fits management's plan to remove friction from the free tier (see question 2). This is an inference, not stated.
- DET (+2%) is no longer a growth line. At $9.9M a quarter it is about 3% of revenue.

### Gaps
- I did not read the Q3 2026 earnings call transcript. Management's spoken remarks on competition, 2027 and DAU growth by region are not covered here.
- No breakdown of subscribers by tier (Super, Family, Max) or of ARPU per paid subscriber was found in the letter.
- DAUs by subject (languages vs chess vs math vs music) were not disclosed for Q3 2026.

## 2. What do they sell, and what is the current sentiment?

### Takeaway
The product is a free, ad-supported app with paid tiers. The company is now giving AI features away more widely: new Max sales are ending and unlimited Video Call is moving into Super. Wall Street turned more positive after Q3 2026, but the consensus is still Hold. User sentiment is mixed: complaints about the Energy system, ads and AI content, while retention metrics improve.

### Cited Findings
- Max is being discontinued for new sales. Existing individual Max subscribers move to a Super family plan at the same price. The company calls the bookings impact minimal and says it is in Q4 guidance. Legacy Max family plans will be handled in 2027 ([Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm))
- Unlimited Video Call (the AI conversation feature) began for new Super subscribers in Q2/Q3 2026 and should reach all Super subscribers by year-end 2026. Management calls Video Call a key subscription driver ([Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm); [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm))

## 3. Competitors, and is AI a threat or a moat?

### Takeaway
No direct language-app competitor is close in scale. Chegg/Busuu is shrinking, and Speak's last disclosed round was a $1B valuation in late 2024. The real competitive question is general AI: Google Translate's free Gemini Practice mode, plus ChatGPT and Gemini as tutors. The evidence is two-sided. Reviewers say Translate is less structured than Duolingo, and Duolingo's DAU growth reaccelerated in 2026 even as these tools spread.

### Cited Findings
- Google Translate launched an AI "Practice" mode (Gemini-powered, personalized listening and speaking sessions), first for English speakers learning Spanish and French and vice versa ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/apps/google-translates-latest-feature-is-its-take-on-duolingo-160035157.html)). For Translate's 20th anniversary in 2026, Google added pronunciation practice on Android (US and India first; English, Spanish, Hindi) and reported 1B monthly users for Translate ([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/-MI53J0B6tLPsnrZsUN5); secondary source)
- Reviewers: Android Authority says Translate's version is "surprisingly good" but much less structured than Duolingo ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-translate-vs-duolingo-3603240/)). A 2026 overview calls it a free practice option, not a replacement for a course ([AI Chat Daily](https://www.aichatdaily.com/tools/google-translate))
- Duolingo's CEO argues that LLMs like ChatGPT and Gemini lack the gamified elements that keep users coming back (search summary of [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/apps/google-translates-latest-feature-is-its-take-on-duolingo-160035157.html) and related coverage)
- Speak (OpenAI-backed, AI speaking practice): $78M Series C at a $1B valuation, led by Accel, Dec 2024 (older than 6 months) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/openai-backed-speak-raises-78m-at-1b-valuation-to-help-users-learn-languages-by-talking-out-loud)). Latka reports $100M revenue in Nov 2025 ([Latka](https://www.getlatka.com/companies/speak.com)); Multiples.vc lists $50M (Dec 2024). Low confidence. No 2026 round was found.
- Chegg (owns Busuu): Q3 2026 revenue $46.5M, -40% year over year; adjusted EBITDA $5.4M; $43.6M cash, no debt. Q4 revenue guide $38-40M. Its "Skilling" segment, which includes Busuu, should exceed $65M revenue in 2026. Management says language learning is "returning to growth" in B2B and B2C. No Busuu revenue was disclosed ([Chegg Q3 2026 release, GlobeNewswire, 2026-11-03](https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/03/3394197/0/en/chegg-reports-third-quarter-2026-earnings.html); [Finsee](https://finsee.ai/earnings/chgg/2026/q3/en/))
- One analysis claims Duolingo has about 85% of all language-learning app DAUs worldwide (search summary of [Potential Multibaggers](https://www.potentialmultibaggers.com/p/duolingo-uncertainty-and-vision); third-party estimate, method unknown)
- Duolingo's filings list "competition in the online language learning industry" and "the success of our development, implementation and use of artificial intelligence" as risks. The Q3 letter does not discuss competitors by name ([Q3 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069692/duol-20260930.htm))

### Inferences
- The data does not show AI cutting into Duolingo's user base in 2026. DAU growth went from 21% to 23% to 29% during the year that Gemini Practice and voice chatbots spread. A threat to pricing power (why pay for Super when Translate practice is free?) fits the evidence equally well: Duolingo is giving AI features away, and paid penetration is flat.
- Chegg's 40% revenue drop is the cautionary example of a subscription education business that general AI hollowed out. Chegg sold homework answers, which ChatGPT replaces directly. Duolingo sells habit and motivation, which is harder to replace. This is the core of the debate, not a settled fact.

### Gaps
- Babbel 2026 financials, Rosetta Stone (IXL) and Busuu user numbers: not found.
- No data on how many people use ChatGPT or Gemini for language learning.
- No independent 2026 market-share data (Sensor Tower or Similarweb).

## 4. Financial growth vs competitors and comparable subscription apps

### Takeaway
Duolingo is still growing faster than mature consumer subscription peers (14% revenue, 12% bookings vs Spotify 16%/14% constant currency, Match -3%, Chegg -40%), with much higher gross margin than Spotify. But its growth went from about 40% to the low teens in a year, and adjusted EBITDA margin fell about 4 points to fund free-user growth. GAAP profit is now fully taxed, so year-over-year GAAP comparisons look terrible because of a 2025 tax benefit.

### Cited Findings
- Duolingo Q3 2026: gross margin 72.9% (+40 bps year over year, helped by lower AI cost per feature and hosting). Adjusted EBITDA $78.7M, 25.5% margin (vs $80.0M, 29.5%). GAAP net income $27.2M (8.8% margin) vs $292.2M in Q3 2025, which included a $222.7M one-time tax benefit. GAAP opex +24% ($39.2M), including an $11.4M accrual for a company-wide bonus tied to Q4 DAU growth of 25% or more ([Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm))
- AI costs and gross margin: Q4 2026 gross margin is guided to 72.0% (-90 bps quarter over quarter) as AI features roll out more widely ([Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)). In Q2 2026, the full-year gross margin outlook was raised to about 71.6% on "AI cost efficiencies" ([Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm))
- Stock-based compensation is about 14% of 2026 revenue. Dilution before buybacks is 3.5-4.0% a year. Effective tax rate is 23-25% ([Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm))
- FY2025 adjusted EBITDA margin 29.5%; Q4 2025 29.8% (older than 6 months) ([Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012513/q4fy25duolingo12-31x25shar.htm)). FY2026 guide 26.5% ([Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm))
- Spotify Q3 2026 (reported 2026-10-22): revenue €4.969B, +16% (+14% constant currency). Gross margin 33.0%, a Q3 record. Operating margin 13.5%. Premium subscribers 306M (+9%), MAUs 788M (+11%) ([Music Business Worldwide](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-is-on-course-for-a-3-3b-annual-operating-profit-in-2026-after-adding-6m-subscribers-in-q3); [Beancount analysis](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/10/23/spotify-q3-2026-earnings-analysis))
- Match Group Q3 2026 (reported 2026-11-02): revenue $891.2M, down about 2.5-3%. Net income $174M (+8%), adjusted EBITDA $344M (+14%). Payers 13.8M (-5%), revenue per payer $21.19 (+3%). Hinge revenue about +19% ([Webull/StockStory](https://www.webull.com/news/15856414522711040); [Securities.io](https://www.securities.io/payers-fall-5-at-match-group-as-q3-revenue-slips-to-891-million/))
- Chegg Q3 2026: revenue -40%, adjusted EBITDA $5.4M ([Chegg release](https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/03/3394197/0/en/chegg-reports-third-quarter-2026-earnings.html))

| Q3 2026 | Revenue growth | Gross margin | Profit margin | Paid users growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Duolingo | +14% (bookings +12%) | 72.9% | Adj. EBITDA 25.5%; GAAP op. margin about 12.7% (Yahoo) | +14% |

### Inferences
- Duolingo's gross margin (about 73%) is more than double Spotify's because it pays no royalties. AI inference is its main variable cost, and so far that cost is falling per unit (margin up 40 bps year over year). The Q4 guide (-90 bps) is the first sign of AI costs showing up as Video Call goes wider.
- Adjusted EBITDA excludes SBC worth about 14% of revenue. GAAP operating margin (12.7% per Yahoo) is the fairer comparison with Spotify's 13.5%.
- The $11.4M bonus accrual tied to Q4 DAU growth shows management is paying for the DAU goal. That is good for alignment on users, but it is a cost the market should watch.

### Gaps
- Match and Chegg gross margins were not gathered.
- Babbel and Speak margins are not public.
- I did not independently confirm the 12.7% GAAP operating margin; it comes from the fund app's Yahoo pull.

## 5. Cash vs debt, FCF, buybacks

### Takeaway
The balance sheet is very strong. There is $1.48B of cash and investments and no borrowings, about 18-20% of the market cap. Trailing FCF is about $395M on the company's definition. Buybacks have been small so far ($82M of a $400M authorization), mainly offsetting dilution.

### Cited Findings
- At 2026-09-30: cash and equivalents $1,239.6M, short-term investments $134.3M, long-term investments $110.1M, total $1,484.0M. No debt, notes or credit facility in the statements. Long-term operating lease liability $84.2M ([Q3 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069692/duol-20260930.htm))
- FCF: Q3 2026 $75.3M (24.4% margin) vs $77.4M (28.5%). Operating cash flow $89.4M ([Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)). 9M 2026: operating cash flow $328.5M, capex $18.0M, capitalized software $8.7M, FCF $301.8M ([Q3 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069692/duol-20260930.htm)). Q4 2025 FCF $93.7M; FY2025 FCF $360.4M (older than 6 months) ([Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012513/q4fy25duolingo12-31x25shar.htm)). FY2026 FCF was guided above $350M in February ([Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012513/q4fy25duolingo12-31x25shar.htm))
- Buybacks: $400M program announced February 2026. 9M 2026: 743K shares for $76.7M (average $103.21); $323.3M left ([Q3 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069692/duol-20260930.htm)). Through 2026-10-30: 781,439 shares for $82.0M, which management says offsets all dilution from 2024 and 2025. Q3 alone was only $6.4M (48,419 shares). Fully diluted shares were 50.8M at quarter end ([Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm))

### Inferences
- Trailing FCF of about $395M (Q4'25 $93.7M + 9M'26 $301.8M, computed) is about a 5.3% FCF yield on the $7.43B Yahoo market cap. Excluding about $1.48B of cash and investments, the enterprise value is about $5.95B, so EV/FCF is about 15x (computed). FCF includes SBC add-backs (about 14% of revenue), so the GAAP-adjusted yield is lower.
- Management bought most shares near $100 in the spring and almost stopped in Q3 as the price rose. That is price-sensitive buying, not a steady return of capital.

### Gaps
- Interest income on the cash pile in 2026 was not extracted.
- Whether the $0.31B Yahoo FCF figure is levered FCF or a different period is unconfirmed.

## 6. P/E: trailing, forward, and what it implies

### Takeaway
Trailing GAAP P/E of about 52 is real. The 2025 tax benefit is no longer in the trailing four quarters, except possibly a smaller piece in Q4 2025. The forward P/E of 20.1 is not comparable: it almost certainly uses non-GAAP (adjusted) EPS estimates, which exclude SBC. Treat "52 to 20" as a change in accounting basis plus growth, not as a sign that earnings will double.

## 7. Contrarian angles: bull vs bear, guidance changes, drawdown

### Takeaway
The stock is about 71% below its May 2025 high and about 17% below the fund's $190.05 cost basis. Two stories fit the same data. Bull: management gave up about $50M+ of 2026 bookings to win back user growth, and it worked (DAU growth up from 21% to 29%, guidance raised). Bear: users grow but paid penetration does not, AI is free elsewhere, and growth may depend on bonus-driven DAU tactics. A securities class action is pending.

### Inferences
Bull case ("gamified habit / engagement moat"):
- The strategy reset worked faster than promised. DAU growth was 29% vs the 20% target, DAU/MAU rose to 42%, and the 10-Q credits retention.
- Bookings are already reaccelerating: +8% in Q2, +12% in Q3, +13% guided for Q4. Revenue follows bookings with a lag, so 2027 revenue growth could rise from today's 14%.
- AI is lowering Duolingo's costs (gross margin up despite Video Call), and it can bundle AI into a product people already open daily. Chatbots lack streaks, leagues and notifications.
- $1.48B cash, no debt, about 15x EV/FCF, and a buyback that management uses when the price is low.

Bear case ("AI kills language learning" and quality-of-growth worries):
- Users are growing, but payers are not keeping up: paid penetration is flat at about 8.6% of MAUs, ads are flat and in-app purchases -38%. DAU growth is cheap to buy (game partnerships, notifications, a DAU-linked bonus) and may not convert.
- Ending Max and giving Video Call to all of Super suggests AI conversation cannot command a premium. Google gives Gemini-based practice away inside an app with 1B monthly users.
- GAAP P/E of about 52 for 12-14% growth, SBC at 14% of revenue and 3.5-4% annual dilution.
- Q4 gross margin is guided down as AI features expand. The class action and the 2025 credibility hit (third-party data showed slowing DAUs before the company admitted it) are overhangs.
- Consensus is still Hold, with mean targets ($141.56 to $152.54) at or below the price.

What would settle it: 2027 guidance in February 2027, especially whether bookings growth gets back toward the "nearly 20%" management says it gave up; paid subscriber growth vs DAU growth; and Q4 2026 DAU growth vs the 25% bonus threshold.
