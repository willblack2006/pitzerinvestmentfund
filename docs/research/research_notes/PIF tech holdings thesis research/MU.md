## 1. How does Micron make money? (business units and products, Q4 FY26)

### Takeaway
DRAM (including HBM) is 73% of revenue and NAND is 26%. By business unit, data center (Cloud Memory + Core Data Center) is 63% of Q4 revenue. Every unit runs at 79% to 88% operating margin, so this is a company-wide pricing boom, not only an HBM story.

### Cited Findings
- Q4 FY26 revenue $54.2B, +31% qoq, +379% yoy, the sixth straight quarterly record — [Q4 FY26 prepared remarks, 2026-09-30](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- Business units, Q4 FY26 / Q3 FY26 / Q4 FY25 revenue ($M), with Q4 gross and operating margin — [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000723125/000072312526000018/a2026q4ex991-pressrelease.htm); share of revenue — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
  - Cloud Memory (CMBU, includes HBM): 16,283 / 13,769 / 4,543; 30% of revenue; GM 83%, OM 76%; +18% qoq. Margin flat because higher HBM mix offset pricing.
  - Core Data Center (CDBU): 18,002 / 11,524 / 1,577; 33%; GM 90%, OM 85%; +56% qoq
  - Mobile and Client (MCBU): 13,114 / 11,521 / 3,760; 24%; GM 90%, OM 88%; +14% qoq, with lower bit shipments
  - Automotive and Embedded (AEBU): 6,824 / 4,634 / 1,434; 13%; GM 84%, OM 79%; +47% qoq
- DRAM: Q4 revenue $39.8B (73%), +27% qoq, +343% yoy. Bits up mid-single digits and prices up high-teens % qoq. FY26 DRAM revenue topped $100B — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- NAND: Q4 revenue $14.1B (26%), +42% qoq, +526% yoy. Bits up ~10% and prices up ~30% qoq. Data center SSD was nearly $10B, more than 10x the year-ago quarter and over two-thirds of NAND revenue — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- HBM: Micron did not give a dollar figure. It said HBM revenue grew faster than total revenue in Q4 — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- FQ1 FY27 guidance: revenue $61.5B ± $1.5B, GM ~86.25% non-GAAP, EPS $38.15 ± $1.00 non-GAAP. Micron expects revenue to rise each quarter of FY27 and says FQ1 is the gross-margin floor for FY27 — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)

### Inferences
- Revenue growth is mostly price, not volume: Q4 DRAM bits grew mid-single digits while price rose high-teens, and NAND bits grew ~10% while price rose ~30%. That makes earnings very sensitive to any reversal in pricing.
- Higher HBM mix held CMBU margins flat, so HBM is currently margin-dilutive versus conventional DRAM. That cuts against the idea that HBM is the profit engine.

### Gaps
- No HBM revenue figure or run-rate was disclosed.
- No breakdown of the "DRAM made in the U.S." mix.

## 2. What do they sell, and what is the current sentiment? (HBM, pricing, supercycle, capacity)

### Takeaway
Micron and TrendForce both describe supply as tight through 2027 and 2028, with prices still rising but more slowly. Micron has locked in much of its future revenue with take-or-pay contracts. Sentiment turned volatile in October and November: the stock fell on Kioxia's cautious outlook and on fears that TSMC would enter memory.

### Cited Findings
- HBM: Micron has completed agreements for the vast majority of its calendar 2027 HBM bit supply, at significant yoy price increases, which narrows the margin gap with conventional DRAM. HBM4 is ramping. Micron is working with NVIDIA on the first custom HBM4E (NVHBM) for next-generation GPUs and NVLink Fusion — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- Strategic customer agreements (SCAs): 26 signed, estimated at over 35% of revenue through 2030. Three-quarters of that revenue has a defined pricing framework, a majority with floor and ceiling prices. The other quarter is priced periodically at market. Customer financial commitments are $32B, mostly cash deposits. RPO is ~$150B. Management says that even at floor prices, margins would be meaningfully above any prior cycle peak — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf); [TrendForce, 2026-10-01](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/10/01/news-micron-fy2026-revenue-surges-256-to-133-2b-locks-in-35-of-revenue-through-2030/)
- Industry outlook (Micron): DRAM bits grow mid-20s % in CY26 and low-20s % in CY27 and CY28. NAND bits grow low-20s % in CY26 and mid-20s % in CY27 and CY28. Both markets stay supply-constrained in 2027 and 2028, and Micron says it has "no line of sight" to supply-demand balance. It expects a "more moderate rate of price increases" in FY27 — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- TrendForce forecasts 4Q26 conventional DRAM contract prices up 10% to 15% qoq and NAND up 15% to 20% — [TrendForce, 2026-09-30](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260930-13258.html). Another TrendForce article cites ~20% qoq DRAM, which conflicts with this — [TrendForce, 2026-10-20](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/10/20/news-dram-prices-seen-rising-20-qoq-in-4q26-despite-slower-gains-ai-demand-tight-supply-support-outlook/)
- TrendForce says long-term agreements capped server DRAM price increases at 13% to 18% in 3Q26. It expects server DRAM contract prices to keep rising each quarter through 2H27, at a slower pace — [TrendForce, 2026-07-09](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260709-13140.html)
- Capacity plan from the Q4 FY26 remarks: Idaho ID1 wafer output mid-CY2027, ID2 late CY2028. First New York fab had its concrete pour, with initial output in CY2030. Japan DRAM expansion output late CY2028. Taiwan Tongluo shipments mid-CY2027. Singapore HBM packaging output early CY2027, Singapore NAND 2H CY2028 — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- Stock sentiment: Micron fell 4% on 2026-11-02 after Kioxia's cautious outlook, which suggested NAND prices could level off. Kioxia's CFO blamed shipment timing, not demand — [24/7 Wall St., 2026-11-02](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/02/micron-falls-4-as-kioxias-cautious-outlook-hits-memory-stocks-western-digital-drops-5-sandisk-slides-3/). The stock then rose ~6% after TSMC eased concerns about entering memory. The same report said Micron had lost nearly 30% of its market value over the previous month — [Invezz, 2026-11-09](https://invezz.com/sg/news/2026/11/09/why-are-micron-and-sandisk-stocks-rising-today/)
- Shares slipped after hours on the Q4 report as investors weighed heavier capex plans — [Investing.com transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-micron-tops-q4-2026-estimates-as-demand-stays-hot-93CH-4925992); [CNBC, 2026-09-30](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/micron-mu-q4-earnings-report-2026.html)

### Inferences
- The 4Q26 TrendForce ranges (DRAM +10% to 15%) are below the high-teens increase Micron got in its Aug quarter. That fits "still rising, but decelerating," which in past cycles has been when memory stocks peaked. The fundamentals are not yet rolling over.
- The SCA price floors and ceilings cut both ways. They limit downside on about 26% of revenue (75% of the >35% SCA share), but they also cap upside, and the rest of revenue stays exposed to spot and contract pricing.

## 5. Cash vs debt, FCF and capex

### Takeaway
Micron has $73.5B of cash and investments against $5.2B of debt, for $68.3B of net cash. About $12.7B of that cash is customer deposits that must eventually be returned. FY27 capex will rise sharply, likely above $50B.

### Cited Findings
- Cash and investments $73.5B; debt $5.2B; net cash $68.3B. Weighted average debt maturity ~9 years. Rated BBB+ or equivalent by all three agencies after two upgrades — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- Customer SCA deposits on the balance sheet: $12.7B at Q4 end ($12.3B received in Q4). They are unrestricted, reported in financing cash flow rather than FCF, and returned toward the latter half of each agreement's term — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- Operating cash flow: FY26 $89,675M, Q4 $43,973M. Capex: FY26 gross $30,712M, net $27,367M. Adjusted FCF: FY26 $62,308M, Q4 $33,199M — [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000723125/000072312526000018/a2026q4ex991-pressrelease.htm)
- FY27 capex guide: FQ1 ~$11.5B, 1H FY27 ~$25B, and 2H higher than 1H. Construction capex will grow faster than equipment capex. Capex is reported net of expected government incentives — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- Capital return: Micron plans to increase buybacks from Dec 9, 2026 (the second anniversary of its CHIPS agreements) and to return 100% of excess cash over time. The dividend is $0.15 per quarter. FY26 buybacks were only $650M — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf); [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000723125/000072312526000018/a2026q4ex991-pressrelease.htm)
- Inventory: $10.4B, 129 days, up 9 days qoq. Management attributes the rise to build-ahead and capitalized incentive pay — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)

### Inferences
- Excluding customer deposits, net cash is about $55.6B ($68.3B less $12.7B; my calculation).
- 1H FY27 capex of ~$25B plus a larger 2H implies FY27 capex above $50B, versus $30.7B gross in FY26. That is my calculation, not a stated figure.
- Rising inventory days during a "sold-out" market is worth watching. Management's explanation is plausible but has not been tested.

## 6. P/E, price-to-book and the cyclical P/E trap

### Takeaway
At ~$795, MU trades at 10.7x trailing GAAP EPS, about 4.5x forward on StockAnalysis's estimate (3.8x on Yahoo's), and 6.5x book. History shows memory stocks peak while P/E looks low and earnings are still rising. In 2018 and 2022 the stock fell 50% or more before earnings rolled over. The bull counterargument is that even a severe bear case leaves EPS far above any past peak.

### Cited Findings
- Price-to-book is 6.53x (book value $122.35 per share) on 2026-11-12 — [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mu/statistics/). Theta Research puts P/B at about 10x at the March 2026 high, versus a 10-year maximum of about 8x before this cycle — [Theta Research, 2026-03-31 (older than 6 months)](https://www.theta.md/research/mu-cycle-peak-anatomy)
- Past cycles (Theta Research, 2026-03-31) — [Theta](https://www.theta.md/research/mu-cycle-peak-anatomy)
  - 2018: stock peaked at $63.09 on 2018-05-30, at 7.3x trailing and 5.6x forward P/E. Quarterly EPS peaked about 3 months later ($3.56, Aug 2018). The stock fell 56% to $27.70 by 2018-12-26, when trailing P/E was 2.2x. Next-year EPS fell 47%.
  - 2022: stock peaked at $96.29 on 2022-01-05, at 13.5x trailing and 16.7x forward. Quarterly EPS peaked about 6 months later. The stock fell 51% to $47.63 by 2022-12-22. The next fiscal year was a loss (-164% EPS).
  - 2014: stock peaked at $35.70 and fell 74% over 17 months.
- The 2018 stock fell about 56% from its May high while Micron was still reporting near-peak earnings — [Investing.com analysis](https://www.investing.com/analysis/micron-rally-tests-whether-ai-memory-has-broken-the-cycle-200683657)
- In 2018 the stock peaked at about 5x what Micron ultimately earned in FY18 — [Motley Fool, 2026-02-10 (older than 6 months)](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/02/10/how-high-can-micron-go-in-the-memory-supercycle-he/)
- BofA (Vivek Arya) bear case: with DRAM and NAND prices down 30% and 40%, EPS still stays near $100, versus about $12 at the 2018 peak — [Benzinga, Aug 2026](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/08/60911521/micron-bear-case-earnings-memory-downturn)

### Inferences
- The low forward P/E assumes earnings rise from $74 to about $177 to $209. If pricing peaks during 2027, the "cheap" multiple could prove to be the same peak-cycle trap seen in 2018.
- At about 6.5x book, MU is far above its pre-2026 history. Book value is a better anchor than earnings at a cycle peak, and on that measure the stock is expensive, not cheap.
- If BofA's ~$100 bear-case EPS is right, MU would be at about 8x trough earnings at ~$795, which supports the "structurally different" view.

### Gaps
- No full P/B time series for 2018 and 2022 from a primary data provider was found.

## Contrarian angles (both sides)

### Takeaway
The bear case rests on cycle history, record capex, Samsung and CXMT supply, and slowing price increases. The bull case rests on take-or-pay contracts with price floors, $32B of customer deposits, and supply staying tight into 2028.

### Cited Findings
- Bear: Michael Burry's short position is reported as the most prominent bet against MU, and the stock was reported as further above its 200-day moving average than at any point since 1984 — [search summary of bear-case coverage](https://stoxmetrics.substack.com/p/micron-is-not-priced-for-a-broken) (secondary, unverified)
- Bear: the low multiple sits with slowing growth, possibly peaked margins, and a Taiwan labor dispute at the main DRAM site — [Stoxmetrics Substack](https://stoxmetrics.substack.com/p/micron-is-not-priced-for-a-broken) (skeptic view)
- Bear: each cycle's EPS decline has been worse than the last. Record capex makes downturns worse, and Samsung's HBM4 supply plus a 50% capacity increase could compress pricing — [Theta Research (older than 6 months)](https://www.theta.md/research/mu-cycle-peak-anatomy)
- Bull: SCAs (26, >35% of revenue through 2030, RPO ~$150B, $32B deposits). Micron says floor-price margins are above any prior peak — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)
- Bull: data center is over 57% of revenue (vs under 30% in 2018) and there is no channel inventory buildup — [Theta Research](https://www.theta.md/research/mu-cycle-peak-anatomy). Supply is constrained through 2028, and DRAM cleanroom space is the bottleneck — [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)

### Inferences
- The take-or-pay contracts have never been tested in a downturn. Customers could renegotiate when market prices fall below the floors, and about a quarter of SCA revenue is market-priced anyway.
- Most new capacity (ID1 in mid-2027, ID2, Japan, Singapore NAND in late 2028) arrives in 2027 and 2028, the same years Micron says supply stays tight. If demand growth slows, these additions could overlap at the worst time.
