## 1. How does the company make money?

### Takeaway
Two revenue lines: product (hardware arrays and DirectFlash systems, ~58% of Q2 revenue, growing 54%) and subscription services (Evergreen maintenance/upgrades, Evergreen//One storage-as-a-service, Portworx/cloud, ~42%, growing 20%). Q2 FY27 growth accelerated sharply, partly from price increases during the NAND shortage. Hyperscale (Meta plus a second unnamed top-5 hyperscaler) is a separate, mostly future revenue stream with a different model: Everpure sells software and non-NAND components, the hyperscaler buys the NAND.

### Cited Findings
Q2 FY27 (quarter ended Aug 2, 2026), all from [Q2 FY27 release](https://www.everpuredata.com/content/dam/pdf/en/quarter-report/q2-2027.pdf):
- Total revenue $1,185.9M, +38% yoy (Q2 FY26: $861.0M).
- Product revenue $686.8M, +54% yoy (Q2 FY26: $446.3M). Subscription services $499.1M, +20% yoy (Q2 FY26: $414.7M).
- Subscription ARR $2.1B, +20% yoy. RPO $4.1B, +44% yoy.
- First half FY27 revenue $2,238.8M vs $1,639.5M in 1H FY26 (+36.6%).
- GAAP net income $74.1M; GAAP diluted EPS $0.21; non-GAAP diluted EPS $0.70 (Q2 FY26: $0.43).
- Guidance: Q3 FY27 revenue $1.325B to $1.335B (+37% to 38%), non-GAAP operating income $265M to $275M. FY27 revenue raised to $5.03B to $5.07B (prior $4.41B to $4.51B), non-GAAP operating income $940M to $960M (prior $820M to $860M).

From [Q2FY27 prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/687136699/files/doc_financials/2027/q2/Q2FY27-Prepared-Remarks.pdf):
- Core growth "driven by a combination of pricing, mix shift and capacity growth, offsetting lower system unit volumes"; "customers are buying fewer solutions at higher prices"; pricing at "a level that we have not seen in ten years."
- Deals above $5M grew 59% yoy; deals above $20M grew 385% yoy.
- Evergreen//One TCV at an annualized run rate above $1B for FY27; storage-as-a-service TCV +121% yoy to $277M in Q2. Management says Evergreen//One price increases were held "well below" product price increases, and that Evergreen//One acceleration dilutes near-term reported revenue (adjusted, they claim growth "well above 40%").
- Hyperscale revenue was "minimal" in Q2; majority expected in 2H FY27; hyperscale product deployments yield 75% to 85% margins. Second top-5 hyperscaler supply agreement (announced Aug 10) "will begin to generate significant revenue in FY28 and beyond." Hyperscaler commitments expected to extend into calendar 2028 "powering 10's of EB capacity." Company will stop naming specific hyperscale customers and report "Hyperscale Solutions" inside product revenue.
- Geography: US revenue $688M (+19%), international $498M (+75%), international 42% of revenue (a record).
- ARR vs RPO: management says RPO leads ARR because multi-year contracts are booked into RPO at signing.

Hyperscale model and history:
- First DirectFlash hyperscaler win was Meta (late 2024). In the second deal Everpure supplies software and "non-NAND" components; the hyperscaler buys the hardware modules including the NAND; impact first visible in FY28. ([Blocks & Files, Aug 11, 2026](https://www.blocksandfiles.com/flash/2026/08/11/second-hyperscaler-plumps-for-everpures-flash-technology/5285903))
- Q3 FY26 (reported Dec 2, 2025, OLDER than 6 months): company exceeded its full-year hyperscaler shipment plan of 1 to 2 exabytes by end of Q3; said business model choices could affect gross margin. ([Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/pure-storage-q3-fy-2026-results-revenue-up-16-yoy-guidance-raised/))

Longer-term outlook (Financial Analyst Meeting, Sep 23, 2026):
- Preliminary FY28 revenue $7.0B to $7.3B (+39% to 45%); non-GAAP operating income $1.7B to $1.9B (+80% to 100%). Four growth vectors: Core and Core AI, Modern Data Software, Scale AI, Hyperscale Solutions; the three newer vectors expected at ~20% of revenue by FY2030. No long-term margin targets in the release. ([Finviz/PR Newswire, Sep 23, 2026](https://finviz.com/news/394973/everpures-expanding-business-model-fuels-new-long-term-outlook))
- One outlet says Street FY28 revenue estimates were ~$6.2B before the meeting and shares rose 18%+ to an all-time high the next day. ([24/7 Wall St, Sep 27, 2026](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/09/27/everpures-7-billion-forecast-just-blew-past-wall-streets-numbers/); [Nasdaq/Motley Fool](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/why-everpure-stock-popped-today))
- Guidance path for FY27: started at $4.3B to $4.4B (+17% to 20%) in Feb 2026 ([Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/2875839/pstg-beats-on-q4-earnings-sales-enters-fy27-with-hyperscale-momentum)), raised to $4.41B to $4.51B in May, then to $5.03B to $5.07B in Aug ([Q2 FY27 release](https://www.everpuredata.com/content/dam/pdf/en/quarter-report/q2-2027.pdf)).

### Inferences
- Product is now the growth engine (+54%) and subscription is the steadier base (+20%). Subscription share of revenue fell to ~42% in Q2 FY27 from ~48% a year earlier (414.7/861.0), so the business is temporarily becoming more hardware-weighted and more cyclical, not less.
- Because growth came with "lower system unit volumes", a meaningful part of the 38% is price. If NAND prices fall, product revenue could decelerate even if share gains hold.
- The hyperscale model (no NAND on Everpure's books, 75% to 85% margins) is structurally insulated from NAND cost spikes, unlike the enterprise array business.

### Takeaway
Product line: FlashArray (block), FlashBlade incl. //S and //EXA (file/object, high-performance AI), DirectFlash modules (own flash modules built from raw NAND), Portworx (Kubernetes data), Everpure Cloud, plus new data management software (1touch-based Data Intelligence, Data Stream). Sentiment is strongly positive after the guidance raises, with the main worry being NAND costs and pricing-driven growth.

### Cited Findings

## 5. Cash vs debt, FCF, buybacks, stock-based comp

### Takeaway
Everpure has ~$1.0B in cash plus marketable securities and no borrowings; the "$0.23B debt" in the app is operating lease liabilities. FCF swung to -$238M in Q2 because of NAND prepayments; management guides FY27 FCF of $600M to $800M, which requires a large 2H recovery. SBC is high (13.5% of Q2 revenue) and buybacks plus tax withholding have not stopped the diluted share count from rising.

## 7. Contrarian angles: bear case and bull case

### Takeaway
The core debate is whether the 38% growth is durable share gain plus a new hyperscale business (bull) or a NAND-price-driven cycle that inflates hardware revenue now and could reverse, with heavy SBC and a pre-paid NAND bet on the balance sheet (bear). Both sides have real evidence.

### Cited Findings
Bear / skeptic:
- Growth is partly price: "lower system unit volumes", "customers are buying fewer solutions at higher prices." ([Q2FY27 prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/687136699/files/doc_financials/2027/q2/Q2FY27-Prepared-Remarks.pdf))
- In May 2026 (Q1 call) management said it was "unsure about sustainability of demand" after "unprecedented price increases." ([Q2FY27 prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/687136699/files/doc_financials/2027/q2/Q2FY27-Prepared-Remarks.pdf))
- Competitors are seeing the same lift: NetApp all-flash +47%, Dell storage +26%. ([Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/netapp-q1-fiscal-2027-revenue-reaches-record-2-03-billion-as-eps-hits-2-58-and-guidance-is-raised/); [Dell release](https://delltechnologies.gcs-web.com/node/20956))
- NAND is described as ~90% of an all-flash array's bill of materials, which Everpure does not control; argues a vendor truly absorbing 4x to 10x cost increases would show gross margin compression. Source is VDURA's CEO, a competitor, so biased. ([VDURA blog](https://www.vdura.com/?p=15717))
- Customer concentration: selling to a handful of hyperscalers gives buyers leverage. ([MarketBeat / Barchart, "AI storage uncertainty overshadows breakneck growth"](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/2429408/everpure-ai-storage-uncertainty-overshadows-breakneck-growth))
- Kennedy (STH) notes Everpure is not focused on KV-cache storage, an AI hot spot where hyperscalers are buying QLC SSDs. ([Axautik Group / STH](https://axautikgroupllc.substack.com/p/everpures-array-business-impacted)); TrendForce confirms hyperscalers are expanding QLC enterprise SSD use, including for KV cache. ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260921-13246.html))
- Hyperscale timing has slipped before: the second hyperscaler deal produces significant revenue only from FY28, and the company will no longer give customer-level detail. ([Q2FY27 prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/687136699/files/doc_financials/2027/q2/Q2FY27-Prepared-Remarks.pdf); [Blocks & Files](https://www.blocksandfiles.com/flash/2026/08/11/second-hyperscaler-plumps-for-everpures-flash-technology/5285903))
- Q2 FCF -$238M, prepaid assets +$670M in six months, SBC 13.5% of revenue, diluted shares +2.3% yoy. ([Q2 FY27 release](https://www.everpuredata.com/content/dam/pdf/en/quarter-report/q2-2027.pdf))
- VAST Data at $30B valuation with >$500M committed ARR competes directly in AI storage. ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/vast-data-1b-raise-30b-valuation-nvidia-ai-storage))

Bull:
- Guidance raised twice; FY27 now +37% to 38% vs +17% to 20% in Feb; preliminary FY28 revenue $7.0B to $7.3B vs ~$6.2B Street estimate. ([Q2 FY27 release](https://www.everpuredata.com/content/dam/pdf/en/quarter-report/q2-2027.pdf); [Finviz/PR Newswire](https://finviz.com/news/394973/everpures-expanding-business-model-fuels-new-long-term-outlook); [24/7 Wall St](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/09/27/everpures-7-billion-forecast-just-blew-past-wall-streets-numbers/))
- RPO +44% to $4.1B and storage-as-a-service TCV +121%: forward contracted revenue is growing faster than reported revenue. ([Q2 FY27 release](https://www.everpuredata.com/content/dam/pdf/en/quarter-report/q2-2027.pdf); [prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/687136699/files/doc_financials/2027/q2/Q2FY27-Prepared-Remarks.pdf))
- Hyperscale model sells software and non-NAND parts at 75% to 85% margins, so it is shielded from NAND costs and is accretive to gross margin as it ramps; two top-5 hyperscalers signed; commitments "10's of EB" into calendar 2028. ([Blocks & Files](https://www.blocksandfiles.com/flash/2026/08/11/second-hyperscaler-plumps-for-everpures-flash-technology/5285903); [prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/687136699/files/doc_financials/2027/q2/Q2FY27-Prepared-Remarks.pdf))
- NAND spike may help flash vs HDD less than feared and helps Everpure in two ways: price increases flow through revenue, and pre-buying supply in a tight, allocation-driven market can win share from vendors who cannot get NAND. Management says the decision to honor commitments "solidified" customer and channel relationships. ([prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/687136699/files/doc_financials/2027/q2/Q2FY27-Prepared-Remarks.pdf))

### Inferences
- NAND has opposite effects on the two businesses: it squeezes enterprise array gross margin (66.2% product GM, low end of range) but inflates enterprise revenue, while the hyperscale business is shielded. A NAND price decline would likely cut reported growth but raise product gross margins toward the 70% top of range, which management explicitly expects.
- The near-term test is the Q3 FY27 report (date unconfirmed, Nov 18 to 25): revenue vs $1.325B to $1.335B guide, whether hyperscale revenue "rises significantly" as promised, and whether OCF recovers toward the $600M to $800M FY27 FCF guide.
- US revenue +19% vs international +75% means the headline 38% is not broad across geographies; part of the acceleration may be a few large international deals (deals >$20M +385%).
