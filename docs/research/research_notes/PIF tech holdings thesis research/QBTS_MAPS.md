## QBTS Q2: What does D-Wave sell, and what is sentiment?

### Takeaway
D-Wave sells quantum annealing (an optimization-focused approach, not universal gate-model computing) via cloud and on-prem systems, and is now building a gate-model line through the Quantum Circuits acquisition. Its 2025 "beyond-classical" Science claim is contested by classical-simulation researchers; Street sentiment is still mostly Buy but price targets are being cut and the stock is ~73% off its 52-week high.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 Science paper (published ~March 2025, older than 6 months) used 5,000+ qubits on Advantage2 across four graph topologies and claimed advantage over tensor-network and neural-network classical simulation for certain quantum-simulation tasks. — [Quantum Insider deep dive, 2025-03-13](https://thequantuminsider.com/2025/03/13/d-wave-deep-dive-a-look-at-the-quantum-advantage-findings-and-the-questions-that-remain/)
- Critiques: Flatiron Institute / Boston University researchers published a tensor-network plus belief-propagation method reaching state-of-the-art accuracy on workstations/laptops for targeted problem classes; Dries Sels reported similar calculations on a laptop in ~2 hours; EPFL reported a Monte Carlo method with errors below 7%. — [Quantum Computing Report on Flatiron](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/flatiron-institute-tensor-network-algorithm-overturns-historical-d-wave-quantum-supremacy-claim/); [IT Daily](https://itdaily.com/news/innovation/d-wave-claims-quantum-supremacy)
- D-Wave's rebuttal: the classical work does not cover the full range of geometries, sizes, couplings and observables, and fails on hard regimes such as biclique graphs. D-Wave is an interested party. — [Quantum Computing Report on D-Wave rebuttal](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/d-wave-systematically-rebuts-flatiron-claims-reaffirming-beyond-classical-simulation-milestones/)
- Gate-model roadmap: gate-model simulator beta on dual-rail superconducting tech (beta participants include Jülich, BBVA, FirstQFM, FAU); target a 10,000-qubit gate-model computer with 100 logical qubits running >1M operations. — [SEC 8-K Ex. 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001907982/000190798226000180/qbts-20261110xexx991.htm)
- Gate-model timeline from the call: 17-qubit system on track, with 2027 delivery discussions; first error-corrected logical qubit 2028; 100 logical qubits / 1M+ operations 2032. — [gloom.sh](https://gloom.sh/stocks/qbts/transcripts/q3-2026)
- Critics say the gate-model strategy and Quantum Circuits acquisition lack peer-reviewed milestone data. — [MarketBeat (search snippet; page returned 403)](https://www.marketbeat.com/originals/reevaluating-the-sellers-case-against-d-wave-after-downgrade/)
- Kerrisdale Capital (short seller, 2025, older than 6 months) called annealing a niche with limited commercial relevance and later a "commercial dead end." — [Benzinga, April 2025](https://www.benzinga.com/news/25/04/45078865/d-wave-quantum-qbts-stock-falls-amid-valuation-critique-by-kerrisdale-capital)
- Stock fell ~8% on Q3 results (2026-11-10) on weaker bookings and a one-cent adjusted EPS miss (-$0.11 vs -$0.10). — [TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/d-wave-sinks-8-as-q3-loss-misses-and-quarterly-bookings-fall-19-after-quantum-rally)
- B. Riley cut its target to $27 from $40 on 2026-11-02 (Buy kept); stock was $10.09, 73% below its 52-week high; consensus $35 from 17 analysts; lowest Street target $18.36; stock fell from $36.11 to $10.65 over the year to mid-Oct 2026. — [TIKR, 2026-11-02](https://www.tikr.com/blog/b-riley-cut-its-d-wave-target-to-27-its-still-168-above-the-stock-price)
- A later B. Riley cut to $22 from $27 was reported (date not clear in snippet). — [TIKR search result](https://www.tikr.com/blog/92-upside-for-d-wave-quantum-b-riley-still-thinks-so-and-heres-how) (unverified, snippet only)
